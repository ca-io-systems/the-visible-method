The Part Nobody Says Out Loud

THE PROBLEM WAS NEVER DISCIPLINE

You've tried posting consistently before…

You batched. You planned. You lasted eleven days. Then one post felt wrong, and the whole thing collapsed. That's not a discipline problem. It's a positioning problem: you never decided which stories are yours to tell, so every post is a decision, and every decision is a chance to quit. We fix the decision, and the consistency takes care of itself.

"I could talk to a camera all day. I just froze on which of my own stories were worth telling."

She left the workshop with a month of content mapped and has posted every week since without second-guessing.
