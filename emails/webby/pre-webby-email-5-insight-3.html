The Visible Method

You can do this one today.

You can use this one this afternoon. So it's short.

My old process for finding content went like this.

Open the app. Scroll. See what worked for other people.

Feel a bit bad. Close the app. Post nothing.

I called it research. It was doom scrolling with a job title.

Looking outward gives you a worse version of something that already exists.

In a voice that isn't yours. About a topic you don't care about.

Then it flops and you decide you're bad at this.

I do something else now. It has never run out.

Think about the last ten real conversations where someone came to you with a problem.

Not clients. A friend on the phone. Your sister.

The woman at school pickup. Someone in your DMs.

Every one of those is a piece of content.

And a good one. Real question. Real person. You already know the answer.

You did the hard part. You just did it out loud instead of on camera.

Take one. That's three pieces of content.

Tell it as her question. Or as your answer. Or as what most people get wrong.

Scrolling fails for one reason.

Content isn't sourced from the internet. It's sourced from your life.

The internet is only where you put it. Everyone has that backwards.

Try it today. Write down the last ten.

I'd bet you get more ideas in ten minutes than a month of saving reels.
